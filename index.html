How to play

  • Buy units from the shop and drag them from the bench onto your half of the arena (the blue hexes).
  • Your level is the number of units you can field. Buy XP to level up and see pricier units.
  • Collect 3 copies of a unit to combine them into a 2★; three 2★ make a 3★.
  • Units sharing a trait unlock synergies (left panel). Each unique unit counts once.
  • Combat is automatic. Units attack, fill mana, and cast their ability when mana is full.
  • Losing a fight costs HP. The last player standing wins.
  • Augments: at rounds 3, 10 and 17 you pick one of three upgrades that last the whole game: economy boosts, team-wide combat bonuses, or a crest that counts as an extra unit of a trait you already have.
  • Online: combat starts when every player is ready or the timer runs out. The host's tab runs the game, so the host should keep it open.
  • Mythic: from round 9, each round there's a small chance the 10-gold Crimson Phoenix appears in one random player's shop. If it isn't bought, it can turn up again later. Only one exists in the whole game, so whoever buys it first has it. It starts at half strength and grows with the round and its owner's level.
  • Arena rule: every game rolls one rule that applies to everyone, such as cheaper abilities, a richer economy or an empowered trait. It's shown in the Arena panel.
  • Terrain: a few hexes on each half give a bonus to the unit standing on them (hover one to see what it does). Both sides get the same layout, mirrored, and it changes every few rounds.
  • Gold: 5 per round, plus 1 interest per 10 gold held (max 5), plus win/loss streak bonuses.
  • Shortcuts: D reroll, F buy XP, E sell hovered unit, Space ready, right-click to sell. Drag a unit onto the shop to sell it.
